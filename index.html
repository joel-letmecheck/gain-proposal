Executive summary

Actionstep stays. We build around it.

Gain Lawyers already runs its matters on Actionstep — this proposal does not replace it. What Actionstep doesn't cover are the two ends of the client relationship: the journey before someone becomes a client, and what the client can see after they do.

✉

A new CRM

Built for client intake. Every enquiry captured, followed up, conflict-checked and converted, then handed to Actionstep as a new matter.

CRM = the system that tracks every enquiry until they sign.

◎

A client portal

Clients log in and see their own Actionstep matter in plain English: stage, documents, messages and what happens next.

⌘

Website enhancements

Your Webflow site feeds enquiries straight into the CRM instead of an inbox, and your claim calculator becomes a lead engine.

The problems this solves

What this fixes

These are the common pain points in a busy plaintiff practice. We confirm which apply to Gain in the Phase 1 workshops.

  • Enquiries arrive by phone, email and web form into different places — some get answered, some get lost.
  • Following up an enquiry depends on someone remembering to do it.
  • Intake details arrive on paper or by phone and get re-typed by your team.
  • Conflict checks happen when someone thinks to look — sometimes after work has started.
  • Nobody can say which marketing produced which clients.
  • Once signed up, clients call to ask what's happening — every status call is unbillable.
  • Sensitive documents travel by email; clients lose them and ask again.

Plain-English glossary

CRM. Customer relationship management: the system that tracks every enquiry and prospective client until they sign.

Pipeline. The stages an enquiry moves through, from new enquiry to signed client.

Conflict check. Searching every known party to make sure the firm can act before a retainer is sent.

Actionstep API. The official way other software can read and write information in Actionstep. Without it, the portal can't show live matter progress.

Client portal. A secure login for clients, on the web and as a phone app icon.

e-signature. Signing a document electronically, with the signed copy filed automatically.

VOI. Verification of identity for new clients.

Pilot. A trial with a small group of real matters before everyone is invited.

Hypercare. A short, closely supported period after go-live where problems are fixed quickly.

Webflow. The platform your website is already built on.
